Dear Editors of the Journal of Systems and Software,

Please consider “Migration Skills in Practice: A Retrospective Study of Benefits, Failures, and Evaluation Sensitivity” as a research manuscript on AI-assisted software maintenance.

The paper examines a version-pinned plugin-upgrade skill through historical paired records and a more completely archived 64-output comparison. Its contribution is a traceable case study connecting apparent diagnostic gains to operational-contract failures and grading sensitivity. It preserves original judgments, reports the limits of a bounded non-blind human review, and avoids treating heterogeneous configurations as a model-capability curve. The work complements broader skill benchmarks by examining concrete maintenance requirements and the evidence needed to interpret success.

We believe the study is relevant to readers evaluating AI assistance for software evolution and maintenance.
